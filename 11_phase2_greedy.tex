\subsubsection{Generator comparison under a single decoding regime}
\label{sec:phase2-greedy}

The generator comparison of Section~\ref{sec:generators} was originally run under each model's own default sampling settings, while every later phase uses greedy decoding with a fixed seed. To remove the one place where the study used two protocols, the comparison was repeated under greedy decoding on 500 items per dataset.

On medmcqa, the ordering of retained models is unchanged from Table~\ref{tab:generators}, with \texttt{llama3.1:8b} again ranked first at 58.40\%.
Parse-failure rates on medmcqa remain the dominant separator, ranging from 0.2\% to 100.0\%.

On medqa, greedy decoding changes the ordering of retained models: \texttt{gemma2:9b-instruct-q4\_0} now ranks first at 63.40\%, where the default-sampling run placed \texttt{llama3.1:8b} first. The models whose rank changes are: \texttt{llama3.1:8b}, \texttt{gemma2:9b-instruct-q4\_0}.
Parse-failure rates on medqa remain the dominant separator, ranging from 0.0\% to 100.0\%.

Where the ordering changes, the greedy figures reported here supersede the default-sampling values, and Table~\ref{tab:generators} has been updated accordingly. The exclusion decisions are unaffected: the three excluded models fail to produce a parseable answer on a large majority of items under either regime.
